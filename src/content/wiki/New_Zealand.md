**New Zealand** is a medium-sized Oceanian map added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 35.0](/Update_35.0) by **RickD004** — the North and South Islands, with traditional Māori tribal regions as nations, aimed at Oceania players.

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><img src="/images/New_Zealand_map.webp" decoding="async" width="640" height="1168" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="1890" data-file-height="3450"><figcaption>The New Zealand map.</figcaption></figure>

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 20 named [nations](/Nations), including Te Tai Tokerau, Tainui, Te Arawa Waka, Waipounamu, Rakiura and Rekohu. A further 63 names (such as Far North, Whangarei and Kaipara) are held in reserve and used when a game needs more nations than the map defines.

## Details {#Details}

- **Categories:** Oceania, New
- **Nations:** 20 (+63 reserve names)
- **Public rotation frequency:** 5
