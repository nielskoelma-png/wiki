**Ranked** matchmaking pits players against each other in rated games that feed a competitive leaderboard ladder. Ranked **1v1** has been available for some time; [Update 33.0](/Update_33.0) added ranked **2v2** alongside it, with its own ladder, and [Update 35.0](/Update_35.0) added **Ranked Free For All** and **Ranked Clan Wars**.

Since Update 35.0, ranked play needs a **trusted** [account](/Accounts); the Ranked button and modal show a lock icon until you are. Ranked games pay 1.25× [XP](/Levels_and_XP).

## Ranked Free For All {#Ranked_Free_For_All}

- 4–10 players, matched by ELO.
- Played on continent maps with a 20 minute timer.
- The game ends when one human is left: last player standing wins.

## Ranked Clan Wars {#Ranked_Clan_Wars}

- Clan-sized team games: your [clan](/Clan) against up to three others, 2–5 players a side.
- Rated per clan: the clan gains or loses rating, and the clan leaderboard has a sortable ELO column.
- The queue shows how many from your clan are waiting, and the clan Members list shows each member's ELO contribution.
- Clan tags in the game come from the clan wars assignment.

## Ranked 2v2 {#Ranked_2v2}

- 2v2 has its own leaderboard ladder, shown as a separate leaderboard tab.
- Matchmaking prefers to pair [clanmates](/Clan) as teammates.
- PvP spawn immunity lasts 1 minute.
- Games use compact maps only 50% of the time.
- A game ends early when a full team is dead or disconnected.

## Matchmaking {#Matchmaking}

- The live queue size is shown while you wait, and the client reconnects on silent disconnects.
- Matches that do not fill, or that start short-handed or fail to fully spawn, are cancelled.

## Leaderboards {#Leaderboards}

The 1v1 ranked leaderboard is an individual ladder and no longer shows [clan](/Clan) tags in the rank column; the clan tag is a separate, filterable column.
