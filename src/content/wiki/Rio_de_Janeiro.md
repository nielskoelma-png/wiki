**Rio de Janeiro** is a South American map added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 35.0](/Update_35.0) by **Renan Scarpin** — Guanabara Bay, with 30 nations drawn from Rio's neighbourhoods and the surrounding municipalities.

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 30 named [nations](/Nations), including Copacabana, Ipanema, Niterói, São Gonçalo, Duque de Caxias and Ilha do Governador.
