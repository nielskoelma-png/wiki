**Bab el-Mandeb Strait** is a map of the strait between the Horn of Africa and the Arabian Peninsula, added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 35.0](/Update_35.0) by **RickD004** — a balanced two-team map set in the early 1960s, using the region's flags from that period.

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 26 named [nations](/Nations), including Aden, Sanaa, French Somaliland, Sultanate of Lahej, Emirate of Beihan and Assab. A further 15 names (such as Zabid, Audhali and Al Luḩayyah) are held in reserve and used when a game needs more nations than the map defines.

## Details {#Details}

- **Categories:** Africa, Asia, New
- **Nations:** 26 (+15 reserve names)
- **Public rotation frequency:** 5
- **Team rotation frequency:** 10
- **Preferred team count:** 2 (special games)
