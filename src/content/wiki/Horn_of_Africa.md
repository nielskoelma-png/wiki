**Horn of Africa** is an African map added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 35.0](/Update_35.0) by **RickD004** — a pirating chokepoint off the coast of Somalia, plus an inner water system running from the Nile to Lake Victoria.

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 29 named [nations](/Nations), including Ethiopia, Somalia, Somaliland, Djibouti, Eritrea and Kenya. A further 33 names (such as Ash-Shamaliyah, Nahr an-Nil and Red Sea) are held in reserve and used when a game needs more nations than the map defines.

## Details {#Details}

- **Categories:** Africa, New
- **Nations:** 29 (+33 reserve names)
- **Public rotation frequency:** 5
