**Cape of Good Hope** is an African map added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 35.0](/Update_35.0) by **RickD004** — the Cape Peninsula around a large bay with a single chokepoint, with historical South African flags.

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 26 named [nations](/Nations), including Cape Town, Simon's Town, Hout Bay, Cape Point, Robben Island and Seal Island. A further 36 names (such as Helderberg Rural, Muizenberg and Kuilsrivier) are held in reserve and used when a game needs more nations than the map defines.

## Details {#Details}

- **Categories:** Africa, New
- **Nations:** 26 (+36 reserve names)
- **Public rotation frequency:** 4
