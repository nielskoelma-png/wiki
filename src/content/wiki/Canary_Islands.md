**Canary Islands** is an island map added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 35.0](/Update_35.0) by **RickD004** — the Canary Islands off the African coast, with shortened sea crossings between the islands. It is weighted highly in the team-game rotation.

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><img src="/images/Canary_Islands_map.webp" decoding="async" width="640" height="224" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="4000" data-file-height="1400"><figcaption>The Canary Islands map.</figcaption></figure>

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 14 named [nations](/Nations), including Santa Cruz de Tenerife, Las Palmas, Arrecife, Puerto del Rosario, Valverde and Laayoune. A further 67 names (such as Frontera, El Pinar and Agulo) are held in reserve and used when a game needs more nations than the map defines.

## Details {#Details}

- **Categories:** Africa, New
- **Nations:** 14 (+67 reserve names)
- **Public rotation frequency:** 3
- **Team rotation frequency:** 10
