**Ommatrypta** is a Halloween arcade map added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 35.0](/Update_35.0), credited in the release notes to **PatrickPlaysBadly**, **VariableVince** and **RickD004** — an eye-themed map whose nations all have eye-pun names. The release notes describe it as Halloween-only, boosted in public rotation for the holiday.

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 34 named [nations](/Nations), including Pupil, Iris you were here, Lens Armstrong, Eye Scream, The Nerve and Private Eye.

- **Categories:** Arcade, New
- **Nations:** 34
