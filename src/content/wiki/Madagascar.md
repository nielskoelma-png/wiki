**Madagascar** is an African map added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 35.0](/Update_35.0) by **RickD004** — a dedicated Nuke Wars map with two equal landmasses facing each other across the Mozambique Channel, set in the 1980s.

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 34 named [nations](/Nations), including Antananarivo, Toamasina, Mahajanga, Beira, Nampula and Comoros Islamic Republic. A further 29 names (such as Agalega, Grande Comore and Moheli) are held in reserve and used when a game needs more nations than the map defines.

## Details {#Details}

- **Categories:** Africa, New
- **Nations:** 34 (+29 reserve names)
- **Public rotation frequency:** 5
- **Team rotation frequency:** 10
- **Preferred team count:** 2 (special games)
- **Special-game modifiers:** Water nukes (50% chance)
