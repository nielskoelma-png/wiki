**Pulicat Lake** is an Asian map added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 35.0](/Update_35.0) by **Panindhra Tallapudi**, with terrain improved by **RickD004** — a lagoon on the Indian coast with a single entrance from the sea and a pirate island sitting in the doorway.
