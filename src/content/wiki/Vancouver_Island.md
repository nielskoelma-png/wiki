**Vancouver Island** is a North American map added to [OpenFront](/OpenFront.io) in [Update 35.0](/Update_35.0) by **RickD004** — Vancouver Island and the surrounding coast of the Pacific Northwest, with more than 30 nations.

<figure class="mw-default-size" typeof="mw:File/Thumb"><img src="/images/Vancouver_Island_map.webp" decoding="async" width="640" height="487" class="mw-file-element" data-file-width="2600" data-file-height="1980"><figcaption>The Vancouver Island map.</figcaption></figure>

## Nations {#Nations}

The map features 32 named [nations](/Nations), including Vancouver, Victoria, Nanaimo, Tofino, Campbell River and Clallam County. A further 30 names (such as Whistler, Tsawwassen and Ladysmith) are held in reserve and used when a game needs more nations than the map defines.

## Details {#Details}

- **Categories:** North America, New
- **Nations:** 32 (+30 reserve names)
- **Public rotation frequency:** 4
